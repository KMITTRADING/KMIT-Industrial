# Keyword & Entity Clusters — CaCO₃ B2B, KSA/GCC

Copy to `docs/keyword-clusters.md`. Volumes are not included deliberately: in this niche, monthly volumes are low and misleading — a single ranking page can produce a 500-tonne enquiry. Prioritise by **buyer intent and specificity**, not volume. Validate with Search Console after 60 days and revise.

## Market context (for positioning, verify before publishing as claims)

The Saudi calcium carbonate market is a real, contested category. Established local and regional producers include Saudi Carbonate Co. (an Omya joint venture), Astra Mining, National Calcium Carbonate Company, Al-Jazira Factories, and Al Sorayai Group; global reference brands are Omya, Imerys, and Minerals Technologies. Construction is the dominant end-use segment in KSA, with plastics and paints following. Practical implication for the site: **competing on "we are a supplier" is hopeless; competing on grade-level technical specificity, published TDS data, and in-Kingdom lead time is winnable.**

## Mapping rule
