## Product Overview
**One-liner:** KMIT is a Saudi manufacturer and supplier of Ground Calcium Carbonate (GCC), Coated GCC, and Precipitated Calcium Carbonate (PCC) serving industrial buyers across KSA and the GCC.

**What it does:** KMIT mines, processes, and supplies high-purity calcium carbonate (>98.5% purity) across multiple mesh/particle-size grades for plastics, paints & coatings, oil & gas drilling fluids, rubber, and paper/packaging manufacturers. Operations span mining/quarrying, grinding & micronizing, in-house QA/lab testing, and multi-format logistics (25kg paper bags, 1MT jumbo bags, bulk tanker).

**Product type:** Physical product manufacturer/supplier — not SaaS. Sales motion is B2B industrial (RFQ / bulk quote, not self-serve checkout).

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Industrial buyers need a technically-verified GCC/Coated GCC/PCC grade matched to their exact process, fast — but most suppliers gate TDS data behind sales contact and offer no meaningful Arabic technical content.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Global producers (Omya, Imerys, Minerals Technologies) run generic worldwide sites not tuned to KSA/GCC logistics or Arabic-first technical communication.
- Cross-border sourcing means slower quote cycles and higher landed cost than a KSA-based producer.
- Local/regional traders often lack documented certifications and consistent batch QA.

**What it costs them:** Engineering time lost verifying specs manually, delayed production from slow sourcing cycles, higher landed cost from importing rather than buying KSA-local.

**Emotional tension:** Risk-aversion — a wrong material choice affects plant-wide product quality and downstream certifications, so procurement is cautious about unverified or undocumented suppliers.
